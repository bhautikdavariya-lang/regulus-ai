The determination of appropriate foreign supplier verification activities is a combination of those characteristics, and the record must include the combination of those characteristics to make such decisions. The record must also contain the nonbinding recommendations 94, which we believe a supplier’s use of a process or procedure that differs from those required under the dietary supplement CGMP regulation generally would be relevant to an importer’s decision to approve the supplier and to the importer’s determination of appropriate supplier verification activities.
