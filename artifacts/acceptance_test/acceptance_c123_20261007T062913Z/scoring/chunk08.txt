  Q: Which section requires that a device label and device package bear a unique device identifier?
  DRAFT_KEY_CITATION: 21 CFR 801.20
  KEY_ELEMENTS: Labels and packages of every device must bear a UDI unless an exception applies.
  LAW [801.20 Label to bear a unique device identifier.] (a) (a) In general. (a)(1) (1) The label of every medical device shall bear a unique device identifier (UDI) that meets the requirements of this subpart and part 830 of this chapter. (a)(2) (2) Every device package shall bear a UDI that meets the requirements of this subpart and part 830 of this chapter. (b) (b) Exceptions. Exceptions to the general rule of paragraph (a) of this section are provided by §§ 801.30, 801.45, and 801.128(f)(2), and § 801.55 provides a means to request an exception or alternative not provided by those provisions.
  EXEC: OK err=None
  MODEL_ANSWER: The FDA requires that a device label and device package bear a unique device identifier (UDI). This is specified in FDA Guidance: Transfer of a Premarket Notification (510(k)) Clearance – Questions and Answers: Draft Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff [1]. Additionally, if a device is relabeled and required to bear a UDI, the labeler must update the information required by 21 CFR 830.310 via the Global Unique Device Identification Database (GUDID) if the labeler's information has changed [3].
