# S5-062: What are the tamper-evident packaging requirements for OTC drug products?

## Generated answer (final, after validation and any repair)

The tamper-evident packaging requirements for over-the-counter (OTC) human drug products are specified in 21 CFR 211.132(b), which requires that the packaging be tamper-resistant and labeled appropriately. Additionally, 21 CFR 211.132(c)(1)(i) mandates that all tamper-evident features and capsule sealing technologies be identified, and 21 CFR 211.132(c)(1) requires that each retail package be labeled with a statement about the specific tamper-evident features used. These requirements ensure the security and effectiveness of OTC drug packaging. [1] [2] [6] [7]

**[2] 21 CFR 211.132(a)** (supporting)

> (a) General. The Food and Drug Administration has the authority under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (the act) to establish a uniform national requirement for tamper-evident packaging of OTC drug products that will improve the security of OTC drug packaging and help assure the safety and effectiveness of OTC drug products. An OTC drug product (except a dermatological, dentifrice, insulin, or lozenge product) for retail sale that is not packaged in a tamper-resistant package or that is not properly labeled under this section is adulterated under section 501 of the act or misbranded under section 502 of the act, or both.
