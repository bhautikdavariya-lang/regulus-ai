ANSWER: Facilities must register with the FDA before they begin manufacturing, processing, or holding food for consumption in the United States. A food facility is required to submit an initial registration to the FDA only once. The registration must be renewed every other year during the period beginning on October 1 and ending on December 31 of each even-numbered year. If a facility has not had any changes to its registration information since the previous registration, renewal, or update, FDA provides an abbreviated biennial renewal process. Foreign facilities must designate a U.S. agent who lives or maintains a place of business in the United States and is physically present. The registration must be renewed every other year. [1][2]
