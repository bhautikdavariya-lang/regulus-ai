ANSWER: The difference between a Device Master Record, a Device History Record, and a Design History File under the current Part 820 is that:

- A Device Master Record is a record of the device's information, including its design, specifications, and manufacturing details, and it is required to be maintained by the manufacturer.

- A Device History Record is a record of the device's history, including its design, manufacturing, and use, and it is required to be maintained by the manufacturer.

- A Design History File is a record of the design of the device, including its specifications and development, and it is required to be maintained by the manufacturer.
