ANSWER: What are the signature/record linking requirements? [1] The FDA guidance specifies that electronic signatures must be linked to the respective electronic records to ensure that the signatures cannot be excised, copied, or otherwise transferred to falsify an electronic record by ordinary means. [2] Additionally, any changes made to the record, including those subsequent to the 74 See § 11.10(b), must be reflected in the audit trail.
