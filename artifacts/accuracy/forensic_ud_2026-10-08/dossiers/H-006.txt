ANSWER: The additional traceability recordkeeping requirements apply to certain foods listed on the Food Traceability List (FTL), and to foods containing listed foods as ingredients, provided the listed food remains in the same form (e.g., fresh) as it appears on the list. The recordkeeping requirements apply to all persons subject to the Food Traceability Rule, and certain exemptions are available. The consequences of failure to comply with the Food Traceability Rule are prohibited under section 301(e) of the FD&C Act, except when such violation is committed by a farm. The update to the FTL is scheduled for YY.
