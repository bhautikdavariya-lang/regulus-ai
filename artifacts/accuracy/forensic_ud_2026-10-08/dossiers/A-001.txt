ANSWER: The current good manufacturing practice requirements apply to the manufacturing, processing, packing, and holding of animal food as outlined in the FDA guidance. These requirements include:

- Preventive Controls for Animal Food (PCAF) which apply to the manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding of animal food. The scope generally applies to facilities required to register with the FDA under section 415 of the FD&C Act because they manufacture, process, pack, or hold animal food for consumption in the United States (21 CFR 507.5(a)), unless an exemption applies. [1]

- Preventive Controls for Human Food (PCHF) which apply to the manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding of human food. [2]

The guidance also specifies that for facilities manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding animal food, the CGMP regulations in 21 CFR part 507, subpart B, and the hazard analysis and risk-based preventive controls regulations in 21 CFR part 507, subpart C apply. [1]
