ANSWER: The scope of 21 CFR Part 11 is defined in section 11.1(a), which states that the regulations set forth the criteria under which the agency considers electronic records, electronic signatures, and handwritten signatures executed to electronic records to be trustworthy, reliable, and generally equivalent to paper records and handwritten signatures executed on paper. This applies to electronic records, electronic signatures, and handwritten signatures executed on paper. The records in question are those that are maintained and used in accordance with the requirements of this part.
