ANSWER: The supply-chain program requirements apply to an animal food facility, and the receiving facility is responsible for it. The receiving facility must ensure that raw materials and other ingredients for their products are received only from approved suppliers, or if received on a temporary basis from unapproved suppliers, they must ensure those materials are subject to verification activities. 

[1] The receiving facility must ensure that raw materials and other ingredients for their products are received only from approved suppliers, or — if received on a temporary basis from unapproved suppliers — ensuring those materials are subject to verification activities. [2] The receiving facility must have a supply-chain program (21 CFR part 507 subpart E). The receiving facility must determine the frequency of conducting supplier verification activities. The receiving facility must conduct supplier verification activities for raw materials and other ingredients. The receiving facility must ensure that the supplier verification activities are conducted in accordance with the requirements of this rule.
