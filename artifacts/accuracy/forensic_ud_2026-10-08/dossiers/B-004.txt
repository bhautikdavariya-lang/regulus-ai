ANSWER: The FDA requires that manufacturing changes to a licensed biological product be reported in accordance with the tiered-reporting system, which is based on quality risk assessment and risk management principles. If a change is approved, it must be submitted as a PAS (major change) under 21 CFR 601.12(e). If a change is minor, the CP (change plan) may be submitted for a single or multiple related changes, covering a single or multiple BLAs (biological license applications). The CP may also enable greater predictability in the timing of implementation of CMC changes, placing the product into distribution sooner, and improving the product supply chain planning.
