ANSWER: The adverse event reporting obligations created by MoCRA for cosmetic responsible persons include submitting serious adverse event reports within 15 business days of receiving additional information, with the date entered in section G, block 4, of the MedWatch form or on the "Introduction" page for the electronic version. Prompt submission is important for public health reasons. If a responsible person receives a report from another responsible person, they must submit a serious adverse event report to FDA within the same timeframe as any report received from an initial reporter. The deadline for submitting serious adverse event reports to FDA applies to reports received by phone or mail, even if a report has already been submitted [1].
