ANSWER: The net quantity of contents on a drug label must be declared in accordance with the requirements in § 201.62, which requires the statement of the net quantity of contents. This is a general requirement for all OTC drug products. Additionally, the label must include the statement of identity, general pharmacological category(ies), or the principal intended actions, and the bold type, size related to the most prominent printed matter. The requirement applies to all OTC drug products [1].
