ANSWER: The hazard analysis and preventive controls requirements apply to animal food facilities under 21 CFR Part 507. These requirements include the scope of the facility, the audit requirements, and the specific preventive controls for animal food (PCA). The audit must assess compliance with 21 CFR Part 225 and 21 CFR Part 507, as well as any applicable requirements of the FD&C Act and FDA regulations. Preventive controls for animal food (PCA) apply to manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding of animal food, and the audit must be conducted under the scope of the Medicated Feed CGMPs. Preventive controls for human food (PCHF) apply to human food, and the audit must be conducted by a qualified auditor. The sources are [1].
