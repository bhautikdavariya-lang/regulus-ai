ANSWER: A firm using a cloud-hosted LIMS applies provisions from 21 CFR 11.100(a) and 11.10, focusing on the vendor's controls. These provisions ensure the authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality of electronic records, and that electronic signatures cannot be repudiated. The FDA guidance on scope and application affects enforcement discretion by setting the criteria for when electronic records are considered trustworthy, reliable, and generally equivalent to paper records.
