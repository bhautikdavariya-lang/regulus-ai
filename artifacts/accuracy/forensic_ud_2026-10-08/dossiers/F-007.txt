ANSWER: Under Part 117, a food safety plan must contain requirements for hazard analysis and preventive controls, as appropriate to the nature of the preventive control and its role in the facility's food safety system. [1]  

The requirements for a food safety plan include identifying and implementing preventive controls to significantly minimize or prevent hazards, as appropriate. [13]  

The requirements for a hazard analysis include identifying and implementing preventive controls to provide assurances that any hazards requiring a preventive control will be significantly minimized or prevented. [14]  

The requirements for a preventive control management components include ensuring the effectiveness of the preventive controls, taking into account the nature of the preventive control and its role in the facility's food safety system. [19]  

The requirements for monitoring include conducting activities that include verification, reanalysis, and validation of the preventive controls. [15]  

The requirements for corrective actions and corrections include implementing procedures to address the hazards and prevent recurrence. [11]  

The requirements for verification include ensuring the preventive controls are consistently implemented and effectively and significantly minimizing or preventing the hazards. [16]  

The requirements for reanalysis include conducting a reanalysis of the food safety plan as a whole at least once every 3 years. [15]  

The requirements for implementation records include documenting the implementation of the food safety plan. [17]  

The requirements for applicability and status include determining whether a food is subject to the modified requirements. [20]  

The requirements for plant and grounds include maintaining the grounds in a condition that will protect against contamination. [22]  

The requirements for withdrawal of an exemption include approving an order to withdraw the exemption before the order is issued. [23]
