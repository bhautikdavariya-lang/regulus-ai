ANSWER:
21 CFR 70.3(s) defines "area of the eye" as the area enclosed within the circumference of the supra-orbital ridge and infra-orbital ridge, including the eyebrow, skin below the eyebrow, eyelids, eyelashes, conjunctival sac, eyeball, and soft areolar tissue. [1]

21 CFR 70.3(u) states that the hair dye exemption applies to coal tar hair dyes intended to alter the color of the hair, with the exemption requiring that the label includes statutory caution and directions for preliminary patch-testing. The exemption does not apply to coloring ingredients in hair dyes not derived from coal tar. [2]

21 CFR 70.3(v) defines "externally applied" as drugs or cosmetics applied only to external parts of the body and not to the lips or any body surface covered by mucous membrane. [3]
