ANSWER:
21 CFR 700.15(a) states that halogenated salicylanilides, including tribromsalan (TBS), are potent photosensitizers and can cause disabling skin disorders.  
21 CFR 700.15(b) states that any cosmetic product containing these halogenated salicylanilides is deemed adulterated under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.  
21 CFR 700.15(c) states that any cosmetic product containing these halogenated salicylanilides introduced into interstate commerce after December 1, 1975, that is not in compliance with this section is subject to regulatory action.
