

#73 COS-720.4-Q3 | How to Comply | Advanced | section 720.4
QUESTION: We sell one permanent hair dye in 12 shades and one lipstick in 8 shades where only the color additive amounts differ. How many Form 2512 statements, and how should ingredients be listed?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 720.4(c)(6), (c)(7), (d)(1)-(3), (e)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 720.4(e): a separate form for each formulation; a single form may cover shades differing only in color additive amounts, except hair coloring preparations listed in (c)(6), which need a statement for each shade. So 12 hair-dye statements; the lipstick shades may share one. Under (d)(1)-(3), list ingredients in descending order of predominance by weight, by the FDA-adopted name under 701.3(c), else common or usual name, else chemical or technical name.

#74 COS-720.7-Q1 | Records/Evidence | Basic | section 720.7
QUESTION: We mailed our Form FDA 2512 for a hand cream. What will we get back from FDA, and how will we know if something was missing?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 720.7
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 720.7: on receipt, FDA will assign either a permanent cosmetic product ingredient statement number or, where a permanent number cannot be assigned, an FDA reference number. #76 COS-740.11-Q1 | Specific Requirement | Intermediate | section 740.11
QUESTION: We're launching an aerosol dry shampoo. What warning must the can carry?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 740.11(a)(1)-(4)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 740.11(a)(1): 'Warning—Avoid spraying in eyes. Contents under pressure. Do not puncture or incinerate. Do not store at temperature above 120 °F. Keep out of reach of children.' Permitted variations: (2) children's products may add 'except under adult supervision'; (3) glass containers may use 'break' for 'puncture'; (4) 'Avoid spraying in eyes' may be deleted if not expelled as a spray. Also check (b) for halocarbon/hydrocarbon propellants.

#77 COS-740.11-Q2 | Exemption | Advanced | section 740.11
QUESTION: Our hairspray uses a hydrocarbon propellant. Do we need the 'intentional misuse' warning, and are there exemptions?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 740.11(b)(1), (b)(2)(i)-(iv)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: Yes. 740.11(b)(1), in addition to (a)(1), for propellant wholly or partly halocarbon or hydrocarbon: 'Warning—Use only as directed. Intentional misuse by deliberately concentrating and inhaling the contents can be harmful or fatal.' Not required for: (i) foam or cream products with less than 10 percent propellant; (ii) containers with a physical barrier preventing propellant escape at use; (iii) under 2 oz net with metered valve; (iv) under 1/2 oz net.

#84 COS-FDCA605-Q3 | Specific Requirement | Advanced | section FDCA-605
QUESTION: FDA wrote asking which fragrance ingredients in our perfume may have caused a serious adverse event. Must we answer, how fast, and will it be public?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 605(f) [21 U.S.C. 364a(f)]
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 605(f): if the Secretary has reasonable grounds to believe a fragrance or flavor ingredient or combination caused or contributed to a reportable serious adverse event, the Secretary may request in writing a list of such ingredients or categories of ingredients in the specific fragrances or flavors. The responsible person shall ensure it is submitted within 30 days of the request. Under FOIA it shall be withheld under 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(3).
