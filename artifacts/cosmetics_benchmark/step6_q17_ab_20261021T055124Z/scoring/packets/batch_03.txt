

#25 COS-74.2203-Q1 | Applicability | Intermediate | section 74.2203
QUESTION: Our salon brand is launching a cream blush and a matching eye shadow, both colored with FD&C Green No. 3. Is FD&C Green No. 3 permitted in both, and does it need certification?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 74.2203(b), (c), (d); 21 CFR 70.5(a)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 74.2203(b) permits FD&C Green No. 3 for coloring cosmetics generally in amounts consistent with current good manufacturing practice, so the cream blush is covered. The section does not specifically provide for eye-area use, so under § 70.5(a) the eye shadow is not authorized. All batches must be certified under part 80 (§ 74.2203(d)); the color additive label must conform to § 70.25 (§ 74.2203(c)).

#26 COS-74.2208-Q1 | Applicability | Basic | section 74.2208
QUESTION: We're an in-house R&D lab formulating a tinted body scrub. Our supplier offers D&C Green No. 8. Can we use it, is there a use limit, and does the color need FDA certification?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 74.2208(b), (c), (d); 21 CFR 70.3(v)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: Yes. § 74.2208(b) permits D&C Green No. 8 for coloring externally applied cosmetics in amounts not exceeding 0.01 percent by weight of the finished cosmetic product. A body scrub is applied only to external body parts, not lips or mucous membrane (§ 70.3(v)). All batches must be certified under part 80 (§ 74.2208(d)); the color additive label must conform to § 70.25 (§ 74.2208(c)).
