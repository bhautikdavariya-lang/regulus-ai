

#61 COS-700.35-Q1 | How to Comply | Intermediate | section 700.35
QUESTION: Our tinted moisturizer contains a UV filter only to protect the formula's color, and the label says 'with sunscreen.' How do we keep it a cosmetic?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 700.35(a), (b)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 700.35(a): using 'sunscreen' or similar terms, or suggesting the product prevents or treats disease or affects body structure or function, makes it a drug under section 201(g)(1). A cosmetic with a sunscreen ingredient that uses such a term must qualify it by describing the cosmetic benefit. (b): the qualifier must appear prominently and conspicuously at least once with the term, e.g., 'Contains a sunscreen—to protect product color.'

#62 COS-701.3-Q1 | Specific Requirement | Basic | section 701.3
QUESTION: We're designing a 4 oz lotion bottle. What must our ingredient list look like in terms of order and type size?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 701.3(a), (b), (f)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 701.3(a): each ingredient named in descending order of predominance. (b): on an appropriate information panel, prominent enough to be read under customary purchase conditions, in letters not less than 1/16 inch in height, without obscuring design or crowding. (f) alternative: non-color ingredients above 1% in descending order, then those at or below 1% in any order, then color additives in any order.

#64 COS-701.9-Q1 | Exemption | Intermediate | section 701.9
QUESTION: We ship unlabeled bulk shampoo to an unaffiliated contract filler who labels and packs it. Must the bulk drums carry full consumer labeling?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 701.9(a)(2)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: Not if § 701.9(a) is met. A shipment to be processed, labeled or repacked in substantial quantity at another establishment is exempt from the labeling requirements of sections 601(a) and 602(b) in transit and while held there. Because you don't operate the filler, (a)(2) requires a written agreement signed by both parties, with post office addresses, specifying processing, labeling or repacking that ensures the cosmetic won't be adulterated or misbranded.

#67 COS-701.13-Q1 | How to Comply | Intermediate | section 701.13

#69 COS-710.2-Q1 | Specific Requirement | Basic | section 710.2
QUESTION: We started packaging cosmetics last month. What deadline does § 710.2 give, and is it binding?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 710.2; 21 U.S.C. 364c(a)(1)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 710.2: the owner or operator 'should' register within 30 days after beginning the manufacturing or packaging operation. It is part of the voluntary Part 710 program, which FDA stopped accepting in March 2023. The binding deadline is FD&C Act 607(a)(1)(B): within 60 days of beginning, or 60 days after the (a)(1)(A) deadline, whichever is later.
