

#13 COS-71.27-Q1 | Applicability | Basic | section 71.27
QUESTION: No one has petitioned for a color we'd like to use in body lotion. Can FDA list it, or exempt it from certification, on its own?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 71.27
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: Yes. Under 71.27, where a petition has not been received, if FDA has facts showing that a color additive should be listed and/or that certification is not necessary for public health protection, it may list the color additive by issuing an appropriate regulation in part 73 or part 74.

#14 COS-73.2085-Q1 | Applicability | Basic | section 73.2085
QUESTION: We're developing a bronzing face cream and want to use caramel as the colorant. May we, and what restrictions, certification or labeling apply?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 73.2085(a), (b), (c), (d)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: Yes. 73.2085(b) lists caramel for coloring cosmetics generally, including cosmetics intended for use in the area of the eye, in amounts consistent with good manufacturing practice (no numeric cap). It must conform in identity and specifications to § 73.85(a)(1), (2) and (3) and (b) ((a)). The additive and its color mixtures bear § 70.25 labeling ((c)). Batches are exempt from section 721(c) certification ((d)).

EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 73.2180(a)-(e)
