#39 COS-80.37-Q1 | How to Comply | Basic | section 80.37
QUESTION: After we take the certification sample, how must we handle the batch while we wait for FDA?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 80.37(a)-(c)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 80.37: immediately after the sample is taken, the batch must be (a) stored in containers that prevent a change in composition; (b) held under the control of the person requesting certification until certified; and (c) marked, by labeling or otherwise, so its identity is unmistakable and it is clearly not to be used until the requested certificate is issued.

#42 COS-82.6-Q1 | Specific Requirement | Advanced | section 82.6
QUESTION: We make an aqueous mixture of certified FD&C straight colors preserved with sodium benzoate. Can it be certified for food, drug and cosmetic use?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 82.6(a)(1)-(3)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 82.6(a): a mixture with no subpart C or D straight color may be certified if (1) each coal-tar color comes from a previously certified batch unchanged except by mixing, (2) each diluent is harmless and suitable, and (3) no nonnutritive diluent is present except resins, natural gum, pectin and, in aqueous solutions or pastes, sodium benzoate up to 1/10 of 1 percent (plus a food-processing exception).

QUESTION: Can a certified FD&C (subpart B) straight color batch be used in cosmetics, and what must it meet?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 82.50
