EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 700.18(b)

QUESTION: Our vaginal product's container can't accept a seal. Can we get an exemption from the tamper-resistant rule, and how?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): 21 CFR 700.25(d)
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: § 700.25(d): submit a citizen petition under § 10.30, with the envelope marked 'Request for Exemption from Tamper-resistant Rule.' It must include the product name; the reasons compliance is unnecessary or cannot be achieved; the alternative steps available or already taken to reduce the likelihood of malicious adulteration; and other information justifying the exemption.
