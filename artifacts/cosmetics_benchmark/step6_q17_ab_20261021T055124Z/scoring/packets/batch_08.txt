QUESTION: We're a 4-person handmade cosmetics company. How must FDA's GMP rules account for businesses our size?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 606(b) [21 U.S.C. 364b(b)]
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 606(b): the Secretary shall take into account the size and scope of the businesses and risks to public health; provide sufficient flexibility to be practicable for all sizes and types of facilities; and include simplified GMP requirements for smaller businesses, as appropriate, to avoid undue economic hardship, which may include longer compliance times. Separately, 612 may exempt qualifying small businesses from 606.

#86 COS-FDCA607-Q2 | How to Comply | Intermediate | section FDCA-607
QUESTION: We're the brand owner launching 12 shades of one lipstick formula that differ only in colors. What listing do we file, and when?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 607(c)(1), (c)(2), (c)(4)(B) [21 U.S.C. 364c(c)]

#88 COS-FDCA609-Q1 | Specific Requirement | Basic | section FDCA-609
QUESTION: Our cosmetic labels list only our Shanghai office address. Does that meet the FD&C Act contact-information requirement?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 609(a) [21 U.S.C. 364e(a)]
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 609(a): each cosmetic product label shall bear a domestic address, domestic phone number, or electronic contact information, which may include a website, through which the responsible person can receive adverse event reports. A foreign address alone is not a domestic address; electronic contact information or a domestic address or phone would be needed. The statutory note sets effect 2 years after December 29, 2022.

#90 COS-FDCA613-Q2 | Applicability | Intermediate | section FDCA-613
QUESTION: Our facility makes OTC antiperspirants and also non-drug body sprays. Do registration and GMP provisions apply to us?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 613(b) [21 U.S.C. 364i(b)]
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 613(b): a facility described in 613(a) that also manufactures or processes cosmetic products not subject to subchapter V is not exempt from 605, 606, 607, 608, 609(a), 610, and 611 with respect to those cosmetic products. So for the non-drug body sprays, those provisions apply; for the OTC antiperspirants, 613(a) exempts them.

EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 613 (21 U.S.C. 364i)(a)–(b); 21 CFR 700.3(b); 21 CFR 211.1(a), 211.67, 211.68(a), 211.160(b)(4)

The cosmetic-only cream has no codified cleaning/calibration rule; 601(c) applies. Shared equipment must meet drug requirements for the drug product.

#94 UC-COS-011 | Specific Requirement | Intermediate | section FD&C
QUESTION: Our distributor's warehouse holds our shampoos. An inspector saw rodent droppings near pallets. Is the distributor covered by any FDA requirement even though it doesn't manufacture?
EXPECTED CITATION (verified key): FD&C Act 601(c) ("held"); 604 (21 U.S.C. 364) facility exclusions; 607
KEY ANSWER ELEMENTS: 601(c) covers cosmetics 'held' under insanitary conditions, so a holding/distribution site is covered by the adulteration provision. Facilities that only hold/distribute are excluded from the 604 'facility' definition, so no 607 registration. No codified pest-control program rule. Brand as responsible person still bears 605/608 duties.
